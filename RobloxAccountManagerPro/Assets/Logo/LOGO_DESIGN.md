# Roblox Account Manager Pro - Logo Design System

## 🎨 Design Concept

The logo system combines security (shield), multi-user (stacked figures), and gaming (Roblox cubic aesthetic) elements.

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: #0F172A (Dark Blue)
- **Accent**: #06B6D4 (Cyan)
- **Secondary**: #94A3B8 (Silver)
- **Background**: #FFFFFF or #0F172A

## Design Elements

1. **Shield Base**: Security/protection symbol
2. **Stacked Users**: Multi-account management
3. **Cubic Frame**: Roblox gaming aesthetic
4. **Gradient**: Cyan to Silver gradient for modern feel

---

## Technical Specifications

- **Format**: PNG (transparent background)
- **Color Space**: sRGB
- **Transparency**: Full alpha channel support
- **Recommended**: Export at 2x resolution for Retina displays

---

## Usage Guidelines

- Minimum size: 16x16 px
- Padding: 8px around icon
- Do not stretch or distort
- Maintain aspect ratio
- Use official colors from palette
